A simple weekly loop

  1. Take a timed set in Bluebook or another official practice source.
  2. Record the skill behind each missed or guessed question.
  3. Search that skill in the 719-card strategy deck and write one example in your own words.
  4. Use the active-recall quiz two or three days later, then retest the skill under time.

Frequently asked questions

Is this official College Board material?

No. The strategy deck and guides are independent study aids. Use College Board for the current test structure, registration, accommodations, and official practice.

How often should I study?

Consistency matters more than a single long session. Two or three focused sessions each week give you time to learn a rule, apply it, and retrieve it later.
