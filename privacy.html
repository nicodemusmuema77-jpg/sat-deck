Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This page explains how strategy.nicxon.tech ("the site", "we") handles information when you visit. The site is a free, single-page reference of SAT preparation strategies. We try to collect as little as possible.

Information stored on your device

The site does not have user accounts and does not send your personal information to us. The following is saved locally in your browser using localStorage and never leaves your device:

  • Which strategies you have checked off as "practiced".
  • Your light / dark theme preference.

You can erase this at any time with the "Reset" button on the site or by clearing your browser's site data. Exporting your progress produces a JSON file that you choose where to save; we never receive it.

Advertising cookies

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. To provide these ads, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies:

  • Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu.

For more detail, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent message before non-essential advertising cookies are used, and ads will be non-personalised unless you consent. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the privacy settings link presented with that message.

Your California privacy rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can exercise the opt-out through the ad-settings links above or by contacting us.

Analytics

The site does not run Google Analytics or any other visitor-analytics or tracking script. GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, may keep standard server access logs (including IP addresses) for security and abuse prevention; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

Strategy cards link to YouTube videos. When you follow a link to YouTube or any other external site, that site's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the content or practices of sites we link to.

Children

This site is a study aid intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@nicxon.tech.
