Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this Digital SAT score estimate?

It is an approximation. The College Board does not publish a single raw-to-scaled conversion table, real scaling changes with each test form, and the adaptive second module means two students with the same number correct can receive different scores. Use it for tracking progress between practice tests, not as a test-day prediction.

How many questions are on the Digital SAT?

The Reading and Writing section has 54 questions across two modules; the Math section has 44 questions across two modules. This calculator takes the total number you answered correctly in each section.

What is a good SAT score?

On the 400 to 1600 scale, roughly 1200 is above the national average, 1350 and up is competitive for many selective universities, and 1500 and up is in the top few percent. Set your target from the middle-50% score range published by the schools you are applying to.
