Study plan generator

Enter your test date, a target score, and how many hours a week you can study. You get a week-by-week plan with specific strategies from the deck to work through each week. Print it or come back to it any time.

The plan spreads the official content areas across the weeks you have left, front-loading concepts and shifting to timed practice near the end. Strategy picks are drawn from the 719-card deck and link to their source videos. It is a starting structure, not a guarantee of any score.
