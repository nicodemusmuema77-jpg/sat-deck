Frequently asked questions

How long should I study for the SAT?

Most students spend two to three months. This generator spreads the official content areas across however many weeks you have left, front-loading concepts and shifting to timed practice near the end.

What should an SAT study plan include?

Targeted content review by section, regular full-length and single-section timed practice, and a review loop where you categorise every wrong answer and re-drill the weakest topic. The generated plan links specific strategies from the deck for each week.

How many hours a week should I study for the SAT?

Four to eight hours a week is typical. Enter your real availability: the plan scales the number of practice sets and strategy picks to the hours you give it.
