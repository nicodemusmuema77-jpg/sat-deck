Frequently asked questions

How much time do you get per question on the Digital SAT?

About 71 seconds per question in Reading and Writing (32 minutes for 27 questions) and about 95 seconds per question in Math (35 minutes for 22 questions). The timer uses these rates to show which question you should be on.

How is the Digital SAT timed?

Each section is split into two separately timed modules. Reading and Writing is two 32-minute modules of 27 questions; Math is two 35-minute modules of 22 questions. Once a module's time is up you cannot return to it.

Can I move back and forth between questions?

Yes. Within a module you can flag questions and return to them before that module's time runs out. This timer covers one module at a time.

Module lengths follow the standard Digital SAT: Reading & Writing is two 32-minute modules of 27 questions; Math is two 35-minute modules of 22 questions. This timer covers one module at a time. It does not submit anything or sync with Bluebook — it is just a stopwatch with a pace target.
