Digital SAT pacing timer

Run this alongside a practice module. Tell it which question you are on and it shows the question you should be on to finish in time, and whether you are ahead or behind pace.

Module lengths follow the standard Digital SAT: Reading & Writing is two 32-minute modules of 27 questions; Math is two 35-minute modules of 22 questions. This timer covers one module at a time. It does not submit anything or sync with Bluebook — it is just a stopwatch with a pace target.
