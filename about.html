About this site

SAT Strategy Deck is a free, no-login study reference: 719 concrete SAT strategies distilled from public YouTube prep channels, plus a small set of tools for scoring, pacing, planning, and review.

Where the strategies come from

The deck is built from publicly available videos by five well-known SAT prep channels: Penguin SAT Prep, The SAT Gamified, James Lu SAT, SuperTutorTV, and LearnSATMath. The pipeline is:

  • Enumerate each channel's videos and keep the ones about strategy, tips, and technique.
  • Pull the English transcript for each kept video.
  • Extract discrete rules from the transcript — a one-line strategy, why it works, tags, and the section it applies to.
  • Remove near-duplicate rules and keep a link back to the exact moment in the source video.

Every card in the deck links to the video and timestamp it came from, so you can always check a strategy against its original explanation.

The tools

  • Active-recall quiz — turns the deck into a spaced-repetition review session.
  • Score calculator — estimates section and composite scores from a practice test's raw results.
  • Pacing timer — a per-module timer that tracks whether you are ahead of or behind the pace needed to finish.
  • Study-plan generator — a week-by-week plan from your test date, target, and weekly hours.

What this site is not

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the College Board. It does not host official practice material. The strategies are summaries of third-party opinions and teaching, and following them does not guarantee any particular score. Score estimates on this site are approximations for tracking progress, not predictions.

Privacy

There are no accounts and no analytics. Your quiz progress, study-plan inputs, and theme choice are stored only in your own browser. Full detail is on the privacy policy page. The site shows ads served by Google AdSense; those and their cookies are covered there too.
